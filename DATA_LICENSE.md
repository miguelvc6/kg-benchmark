# Data Licensing Status

The root `LICENSE` applies to repository software and documentation. It does not automatically grant rights to every
input record, generated dataset artifact, annotation, model output, or third-party work referenced by the project.

Wikidata structured data is provided upstream under the [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 dedication](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing).
Users remain responsible for complying with applicable law and for respecting privacy, database, attribution, and other
rights that may apply in their jurisdiction.

The project has not yet made an explicit release-license decision for all project-authored classifications, selection
manifests, annotations, reports, and packaged derivative datasets. Until a published artifact distribution manifest names
a license for a file, its `license_id: null` is an unresolved release blocker and must not be interpreted as CC0 or as
permission to redistribute it.

A public dataset release should include a per-artifact license inventory and preserve upstream licensing notices. It
should also state whether editor identifiers have been retained, removed, or transformed.
